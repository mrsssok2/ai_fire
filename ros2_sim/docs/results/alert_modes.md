# Blocking vs non-blocking alert (call / SMS)

Generated by `scripts/eval_alert_modes.py`; raw per-run data in `alert_modes.json`. The default 3-fire layout
(`config/fires.yaml`, 2 s of full spray puts a fire out, 500 ml tank, 25 ml/s pump), 3 run(s) per firmware, same seeds for both
(flame-sensor noise 0%), headless. Times are simulated seconds from the start of the mission (the Arduino's 2 s `setup()` included).

*original* = `fire_sensor.ino` as ported (the call blocks 21 s after every spray burst). *improved* = same pins and timings, but
the call / SMS run as a background job (`program_improved`, launch `firmware:=improved`); not in the real `.ino`.

## What the numbers say

* **Time:** with the 21 s call no longer blocking, all three fires were out after **~35 s instead of ~223 s** (6x faster), because the pump is on
  **60 % of the time instead of 8 %** (one spray burst every ~2.1 s instead of every ~23 s). The original makes 10 calls (one per burst), the improved
  firmware 2 (one at a time; a new call is only queued when none is queued or running).
* **Water is the new limit, not time:** the improved firmware used a little *more* water (479 ml on average vs 430 ml, 3 runs) and in 1 of 3 runs the 500 ml tank
  ran dry with the third fire still burning (`TANK_EMPTY`, 2 of 3 out). The pump runs about the same (17 s vs 19-20 s), so the extra water went into bursts that missed.
  The cause is path dependence of the non-searching firmware: in the burst log of a related run the robot kept driving forward along a slightly different
  heading after fire f1 went out and sprayed past f2. I did not isolate it further.
* The original is very repeatable (223 +- 4 s, 430 ml in every run, ideal sensors); the improved is fast but less repeatable. Only 3 runs per firmware.
* The SMS (gas at fire f3) also stops freezing the motors (the original's motors keep their last command during the 7 s SMS); no SMS was completed in the improved runs (`sms` = 0 in
  `alert_modes.json`), probably because the mission ended before the 7 s SMS finished; I did not check.
