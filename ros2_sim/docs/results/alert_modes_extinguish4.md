# Blocking vs non-blocking alert (call / SMS)

Generated by `scripts/eval_alert_modes.py`; raw per-run data in `alert_modes_extinguish4.json`. The default 3-fire layout
(`config/fires.yaml`, 4 s of full spray puts a fire out, 500 ml tank, 25 ml/s pump), 2 run(s) per firmware, same seeds for both
(flame-sensor noise 0%), headless. Times are simulated seconds from the start of the mission (the Arduino's 2 s `setup()` included).

## What the numbers say

With the default 4 s of full-strength spray per fire the 500 ml tank (20 s of pump) is not enough for the three fires, with either firmware (2 runs each, ideal sensors):
the original ran dry after **267 s with 2 of 3 fires out**, the improved after **28 s with 1 of 3 out** (same 500 ml used). So the non-blocking alert
makes the robot ~10x faster but does not make the mission succeed here; it is the same water, aimed by the same non-searching firmware, spent in a tenth of the time.
Results near the tank limit are timing-sensitive: a diagnostic run of the original (not in the table) put out f1 about 25 s later than these two runs.
The faster, more complete comparison with 2 s per fire is in [alert_modes.md](alert_modes.md).
